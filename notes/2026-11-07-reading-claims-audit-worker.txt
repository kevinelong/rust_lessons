## What is now fragile

- The test reads docs/reading.md by heading convention: a block must start with "### E<n>",
  carry "- Status:" and "- Seen:", and quote with "> " lines of 40+ characters. Reformatting
  the register without those markers fails the test - which is intended, but it means an
  editor must keep the block shape.
- "Read" for E1 is pp. 1-9 of 44. The review's findings sections (S4 onward) are not read; the
  block says so, and the claim it supports was chosen to fit what was read. A future claim
  about results would need S4 before it could use [E1].
- The finding that bites is outside this task: Margulieux & Catrambone's gain needed a labelled
  worked example, not just labelled text, so the Subgoals blocks in sections 07, 08, 11 and 16
  are justified only beside such an example. Queued as `subgoal-example-pairing`.
