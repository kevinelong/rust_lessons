## What is now fragile

- The report's column widths live in seven constants in `src/scoring.rs`; the header and each
  row share them, and the test pins the rendered cells, so a width change is a red test, not a
  silently misaligned table.
- Percentage rounding is `{:.1}` on an f64; the pinned fixture values are far from a rounding
  boundary except section 03's mean (19.65 s prints 19.6 because the f64 is 19.6499…). A
  future session whose true value sits exactly on `.x5` would round by binary representation.
